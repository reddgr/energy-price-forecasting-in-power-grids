# Energy Price Forecasting in Power Grids

This repository shares part of my ongoing research activity on electricity markets, time-series forecasting, energy analytics, and AI-driven methodologies for power systems.

Work here includes:
- day-ahead electricity price forecasting across global markets,
- electrical load forecasting and harmonized datasets,
- integration of weather data and other exogenous variables, focusing first on Spain (AEMET OpenData),
- descriptive analytics for energy systems,
- and deep-learning approaches for market prediction and analysis.

The repository serves as a public workspace for exploratory notebooks, data collection pipelines, preprocessing workflows, forecasting experiments, API integrations, and research-oriented implementations related to ongoing investigations and publications. The content changes frequently and represents selected snapshots rather than a comprehensive catalog.

---

# Research Context

The main objective of this research line is the development of:
- standardized datasets,
- reproducible data pipelines,
- and forecasting models

for electricity markets characterized by:
- high volatility,
- renewable-energy integration,
- nonlinear temporal dynamics,
- and strong interactions between price, demand, and weather conditions.

Work in this line includes:
- day-ahead electricity price forecasting,
- load forecasting,
- descriptive market analysis,
- multi-country electricity-market datasets,
- weather-aware energy analytics,
- and hybrid deep-learning architectures based on Transformer and recurrent networks.

The repository also supports ongoing research on:
- cyber-physical energy systems,
- market intelligence,
- resilience analysis,
- and data-driven operational decision support for modern power grids.

# Main Research Topics

## Electricity Price Forecasting

Research on forecasting methodologies for day-ahead electricity markets using:
- Transformer architectures,
- BiLSTM,
- LSTM,
- GRU,
- hybrid deep-learning frameworks,
- and comparative machine-learning approaches.

## Global Electricity Market Datasets

Development and analysis of harmonized datasets for:
- electricity prices,
- electrical load,
- and market variables

across multiple countries and energy markets.

## Weather and Exogenous Variables

Ongoing work on integrating:
- temperature,
- wind,
- precipitation,
- solar radiation,
- renewable penetration,
- and additional market signals

into forecasting and descriptive-analysis pipelines.

## Energy Market Analytics

Exploratory and descriptive analysis of:
- market volatility,
- seasonal behavior,
- temporal structure,
- correlations between markets,
- and interactions between weather and electricity systems.

# Related Papers and Datasets

## Datasets

### UniElecPrice — Unified Cross-Regional Time-Series Day-Ahead Electricity Price Dataset

A global harmonized dataset of day-ahead electricity prices across Asia, Europe, North America, South America, and Oceania.

- IEEE Data Descriptions publication:  
  M. H. Ullah, S. M. Reza, L. M. Gundapaneni, P. Balachander, B. Babaiahgari and A. A. A. Khan,  
  ["Descriptor: Unified Cross-Regional Time-Series Day-Ahead Electricity Price Dataset (UniElecPrice)"](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11169754),  
  *IEEE Data Descriptions*, vol. 2, pp. 329–339, 2025.  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEEDATA.2025.3609683

- Mendeley Data repository:  
  Md Habib Ullah, Sayed Mohsin Reza, Lasya Madhuri Gundapaneni, Pranav Balachander, Bhanu Babaiahgari, Abdullah Al Ahad Khan,  
  ["Global Day-Ahead Electricity Price Dataset"](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/s54n4tyyz4/3),  
  *Mendeley Data*, V3, 2025.  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.17632/s54n4tyyz4.3

### GloElecLoad — Global Electricity Markets Load Forecasting and Planning Dataset

A standardized worldwide dataset for electrical load forecasting and comparative energy-market analysis.

- IEEE Data Descriptions publication:

  ["Descriptor: Global Electricity Markets Load Forecasting and Planning Dataset (GloElecLoad)"](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11489469),  
  *IEEE Data Descriptions*, Early Access, 2026.  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEEDATA.2026.3686770

- IEEE DataPort dataset repository:  
  Md Habib Ullah, Sayed Mohsin Reza, Abdullah Al Ahad Khan, Siraj Us Salekin, Ali M. S. Al-Bayati, Bhanu Babaiahgari, David González Romero, Jesús Rodríguez Molina,

  *IEEE DataPort*, 2026.
